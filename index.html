About Hako

Hako (箱) is a personal life-strategy tracker built around the three-bucket framework from this video by Knowspire. Sibling app to Midori.

All your data lives in your browser (LocalStorage). Nothing leaves your device. Clear your browser data to reset, or use the buttons below to export/import.

Data portability

Export your buckets, logs, and reflections to a JSON file, or restore from a backup.

Reset

Wipe all stored data and reseed the bucket structure. This cannot be undone.
